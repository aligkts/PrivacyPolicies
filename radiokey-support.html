Common questions

Where do I find the serial number?

Each radio in the app has a step-by-step guide. The serial is usually on a label on the side or top of the radio, which you see after removing it from the dashboard, or on a card in the car's documents.

Do I pay if no code is found?

No. The app checks the serial for free first. You only pay once a code has been found.

I paid but did not get my code.

Open the app again while online, or tap "Restore purchase": the app finishes any pending unlock. Paid codes stay under "My codes".

The code does not work.

Check that the radio model and every character of the serial are correct, then look up the code again (a serial already paid for is free). If it still fails, email us with the model and the last three characters of the serial.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Email us with the radio model and the last three characters of the serial; we delete the matching records. See the privacy policy.
