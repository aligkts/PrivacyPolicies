Hookmate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Hookmate turns crochet and knitting patterns you already have into rows you can follow. This policy says what the app sends, what it keeps and where.

Who is responsible

Hookmate is published by Ali Göktaş, İstanbul, Türkiye, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: alicious.apps@gmail.com.

No account needed

You don't sign up. The app signs in anonymously and gets a random id, which is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

What is sent to our service

  • Patterns you import. The link, PDF or photos you choose are sent to our service and read by Anthropic's Claude to turn them into rows. They are not stored after the import is done.
  • Text you translate (Pro). The pattern text is sent to our service and translated by Anthropic's Claude. It is not stored on the server.

Our service runs on Supabase (United States). Anthropic processes the content only to answer the request.

What stays on your phone

Your projects, row and stitch progress, edits and the photos of imported pages are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What is stored on our server

Only your anonymous id, counters (how many imports and translations you used, so the free plan and the daily limits work) and, if you buy Pro, whether Pro is active and until when.

Purchases

Hookmate Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat checks your subscription with your anonymous id. We never see your payment details.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) to learn which features are used and to fix crashes. The events we send carry kinds and counts only, such as "an import from a PDF finished with 40 rows". They never contain pattern text, links, project names or photos, and no advertising id is collected. Like any app using Firebase, Google also receives basic technical data: the device model, operating system and app version, an app-instance id and an approximate location derived from the network.

There is no advertising in Hookmate, and no data is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Legal basis and transfers

Imports, translations and purchases are processed to provide the service you ask for. Analytics and crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. The services above may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

Children

Hookmate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights and deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. To get a copy of, or delete, your anonymous id and its counters on our server, write to alicious.apps@gmail.com and we will do it. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

To delete your Hookmate data: 1. Email alicious.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hookmate data". 2. We delete your anonymous id, its usage counters and its Pro status record within 30 days and reply to confirm. Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules. Analytics and crash data are not linked to you and expire under Firebase's retention settings. Uninstalling the app deletes your projects on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
