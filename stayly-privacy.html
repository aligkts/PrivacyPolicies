Stayly

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Stayly counts the days you spend in each country. Your days and your locations stay on your phone. This policy covers Stayly for iPhone and for Android. Stayly has no account, shows no ads and does not track you.

Who is responsible

Stayly is published by Ali Göktaş, İstanbul, Türkiye ("we"). For any privacy question or request, write to alicious.apps@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Location. If you turn on automatic tracking, Stayly uses your phone's location services (on Android, approximate location only) to work out which country you are in. Only the country and the date are saved, in the app's storage on your phone. Your location, the countries and the dates are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos. If you import past trips from photos, Stayly reads only the date and place stored in the photos you allow it to see (on Android, the photos or folder you pick for that import). The photos and their places are not uploaded.
  • Your days, rules, settings and reports. Stored only on your phone. A PDF or CSV export leaves the app only when you share it yourself.
  • Alerts. Limit alerts are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

On iPhone, your device may include app data in its own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. That backup is handled by Apple under your Apple account, not by us. On Android, Stayly turns Android backup off, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

What leaves your phone

Three services receive limited data. None of it contains a country, a date of travel or a place, and none of it is linked to your name, Apple account or Google account.

Crash reports (always on)

When the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report: the stack trace, device model, iOS or Android version, app version and a random installation ID. We use it only to find and fix bugs. Crash reports contain no location data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app.

Anonymous usage statistics (off unless you turn them on)

If you turn on "Share anonymous usage statistics" during setup or in Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase receives a few app events: setup finished (number of rules, whether tracking is on), paywall shown, purchase completed (yearly or lifetime), export created (PDF or CSV), photo trips saved (count), tracking turned on, and rule added (rule type). It also receives a random app-instance ID and, from your IP address, a general region that Google derives itself. Events never include countries, dates or places. You can turn this off at any time in Settings; nothing more is sent after that. Legal basis: your consent.

Purchases

Stayly Pro is sold through the App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID so the app can tell whether you have Pro and restore it on a new phone. Legal basis: performing the purchase you asked for.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android) and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No account, no name, no email address and no contacts collected in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Service providers

  • Google LLC — Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — purchase status. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple — App Store payments and subscriptions. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google — Google Play payments and subscriptions. policies.google.com/privacy

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you edit it or delete the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
  • Crash reports: kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: kept by Google Analytics for 2 months.
  • Purchase records: kept by RevenueCat and Apple or Google as long as needed to provide Pro and as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn location access off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Stayly.
  • On Android, turn location access off in Settings → Apps → Stayly → Permissions → Location.
  • Choose which photos Stayly can see, or none, in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. On Android, Stayly sees only what you pick for each import.
  • Turn usage statistics off in Stayly → Settings.
  • Delete all your days by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the data we receive carries no name or account, we usually cannot tell which records are yours; turning off usage statistics or deleting the app stops all further data. Write to alicious.apps@gmail.com.

Children

Stayly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it applies.
