Support

Questions, a pattern that came out wrong or an idea? Write to alicious.apps@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, iOS or Android version, and where the pattern came from (a link, a PDF or photos).

Importing patterns

What can I import?

A written pattern you already have: a PDF, photos or screenshots of a book or magazine page (up to 10 pages), or a link to a blog, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube post that contains the written pattern or links to it. You can also share a link, PDF or photos to Hookmate from another app.

"We couldn't find a written pattern there"

Hookmate only reads patterns; it never makes them up. A video without the written pattern in its description or a linked page can't be imported. Nothing is counted against your imports when this happens.

A row looks wrong

Tap the row and choose Edit row to fix the instruction or the stitch count. Rows marked "Unclear in the source" are worth checking against the original. When the stitches of a row don't add up to the count the pattern states, Hookmate shows a note so you can double-check. "Reset to source" brings back the imported text.

Stitch terms and language

Choose US or UK crochet terms, or stitch names in English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Español, Türkçe, Polski or Nederlands, in Settings → Stitch terms.

Following a pattern

Open a pattern and tap Follow. Tap for the next row, count stitches with the counter, and pick up exactly where you left off. The row counter is also on the lock screen (iOS Live Activity, Android notification). Volume buttons can move to the next row and add a stitch: turn them on in Settings.

Hookmate Pro

What does Pro include?

Unlimited imports (up to 40 a day), translation of patterns into your language, and as many active projects as you like. The free plan includes 3 imports and one active project.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hookmate. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hookmate. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or by Google Play: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.

Your data

Your projects and progress are stored only on your phone, and imported files are not kept on our server. Read the privacy policy. To delete your anonymous id and counters from our server, write to the address above.
